Northstack Console Account recovery

Lost your authenticator?

Enter one of the 10 backup codes you saved when you turned on two-factor authentication for ops@northstack.io.

Letters and numbers only. The dash is added for you.

Each code works only once. Using it removes 2FA from your lost device. Anyone with your codes can do the same, so keep the rest offline.

What happens next

  1. 1

    We verify your code

    It's checked against your saved set and burned immediately.

  2. 2

    Your old device is unlinked

    All other sessions are signed out and your team admins are notified.

  3. 3

    You set up 2FA again

    Scan a fresh QR code and download a new set of backup codes.

No codes? Start identity verification — takes 1–3 business days.
